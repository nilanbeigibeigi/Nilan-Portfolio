I grew up in Tehran and rebuilt my life in Vancouver five years ago. Moving countries taught me to adapt fast, learn independently, and not wait for perfect conditions — the same instincts I bring to a new codebase or a messy dataset.

My approach to work is simple: I look at how the work actually happens, then redesign the workflow underneath it. I'm not satisfied finishing a task for the sake of finishing — I want to know how it could be faster, smarter, and worth more over time.

I think of AI as a multiplier, not a shortcut: idea → system → product → real use. That's why my projects lean toward tools people can actually run, not portfolio pieces built to look impressive and do nothing.
