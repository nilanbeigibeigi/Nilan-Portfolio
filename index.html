Athena — AI Data Analyst Agent

An AI assistant that answers plain-English questions about a business's numbers — ask "which region makes the most money?" and it replies in clear language and draws the chart. It runs in two modes: an offline, rule-based mode that works instantly with no setup, and a real-AI mode that plugs into an LLM (via a free Groq key) to handle open-ended questions. Like ChatGPT, but focused on one company's sales data — with charts.

AI assistantNatural-language Q&AChartsLLM (Groq) + offline mode
Case study

Problem. Non-technical owners can't get answers from their sales data without an analyst or wrestling with spreadsheets.

Approach. A chat interface that turns plain-English questions into queries over the data and replies with a written answer plus a chart. Two modes: an offline rule-based engine for instant, zero-setup answers, and a real-LLM mode (Groq) for open-ended questions.

Result. Anyone can ask "which region makes the most money?" and get an answer and a visual in seconds — no analyst, no setup, and it upgrades to full AI with one key.

What I'd improve. Connect a live database, add query validation and guardrails, and support multi-turn follow-ups with memory.

05 About

I'm not the loudest person in the room — I'm the one who notices what's slow and quietly fixes it.

I grew up in Tehran, where I spent about thirteen years at the piano and guitar, trained as a runner and in archery, and showed work in group painting exhibitions. I've also been around computers since I was a kid — ICDL and E-Kids in Iran, computing classes in high school on both sides of the world — so technology never felt foreign to me. It felt like home.

Five years ago I moved to Vancouver and rebuilt everything from zero: the language, the friends, the way I work. That habit of rebuilding is exactly how I approach systems now. I look at how the work actually happens, then redesign the workflow underneath it — usually without anyone noticing until it's just faster.

These days I focus on data and AI — dashboards that change a decision, AI tools and automations that save real hours, and content that makes AI easier to understand — and I can build the full app around them when it's needed. I taught myself most of it by shipping, all the way back to the small games I wrote in Python in high school here in Canada. Discipline from sport and music, creativity from art and film — that mix is the whole point of how I work.

What's next: land a role that puts my BCIT training to work, then grow into cybersecurity — and, further out, explore where AI meets neuroscience. I'd rather be genuinely excellent in one field than spread thin across everything.
